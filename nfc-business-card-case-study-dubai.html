Workflow Breakdown

1. The Problem With Standard Hand-Offs

A plain card or a raw social link often fails to create a strong digital follow-up. Important contact details get lost, and the brand impression becomes fragmented.

2. The Smart Contact Layer

The solution combines NFC, QR fallback, and a polished web profile that presents contact details, social links, business identity, and save-contact action in one controlled flow.

3. What Changes For The User

Instead of asking someone to type numbers manually, the interaction becomes immediate. The person taps or scans, sees a branded page, and can save the contact instantly.

4. Why It Matters Commercially

The value is not the chip alone. It is the way the contact experience becomes cleaner, more memorable, and more aligned with a premium business presentation.

Case Study FAQ

Why pair physical NFC hardware with a printed QR fallback?
Most modern phones open the profile immediately with one NFC tap, while the engraved or printed QR guarantees instant compatibility on every other handset without requiring any app.
What actions live on a custom NFC smart contact page?
Profiles combine one-tap save-contact (.vcf), direct WhatsApp messaging, website links, social profiles, location details, and custom campaign actions.
