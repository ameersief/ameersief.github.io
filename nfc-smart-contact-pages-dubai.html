Execution Methodology

1. A Better First Impression Than Paper Alone

Instead of handing over a card that can get lost, this service turns the contact moment into a live digital profile. One tap or scan can open the full contact experience with details, links, and a direct save-contact action.

2. Works With NFC Or QR

If a device supports NFC, the card or bracelet can open instantly. If not, the printed QR provides a direct fallback path to the same landing page, so the experience remains smooth for almost every visitor.

3. Flexible For Different Businesses

These smart profiles can include brand visuals, quick contact buttons, location, booking links, WhatsApp, social accounts, lead capture, and any custom features needed for the client's business model.
